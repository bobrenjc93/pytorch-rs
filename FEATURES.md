# Feature coverage contract

Feature completeness is measured against stable public PyTorch Python concepts, with supported claims backed by executable side-by-side semantic tests. Documentation, Rust-only capabilities, and stubs do not count.

Fixed top-level weights prevent easy APIs from overwhelming core gaps:

| Area | Weight | Baseline |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| tensor storage, shapes, strides, views, indexing | 15% | CPU `f32` with Python-owned `Tensor.is_shared()` process-local storage metadata, read-only `Tensor.data` detached shared-storage aliases, read-only `Tensor.name is None` unnamed-tensor metadata, dimension-zero `Tensor.select`/`torch.select` first-axis views, single-Tensor, exact-native-Tensor variadic, and exact-native-Tensor tuple/list `torch.atleast_1d`, `torch.atleast_2d`, and `torch.atleast_3d`, `Tensor.view` with one integer-compatible dimension, exactly two positional integer-compatible dimensions, one shape sequence, or same-dtype `torch.float32`/`torch.float` detached aliases, plus `Tensor.view_as`, arbitrary `Tensor.permute`/`torch.permute` views, integer-axis `Tensor.movedim`/`Tensor.moveaxis`, `torch.movedim`, and top-level `torch.moveaxis` views, strided transpose including read-only `Tensor.T`, `Tensor.mT`, real-valued `Tensor.H`/`Tensor.mH`, and `Tensor.adjoint`/`torch.adjoint`, `Tensor.swapdims`/`Tensor.swapaxes`, and top-level `torch.swapdims`/`torch.swapaxes` views, plus squeeze, view-or-copy flatten/reshape, `Tensor.ravel`/`torch.ravel`, indexing views, and native row-major/channel-last contiguous materialization; cross-dtype reinterpretation, named dimensions and rename/refine-names APIs, shared-memory mutation, and storage-object APIs remain unsupported |
| dtypes, promotion, devices, dispatch | 10% | Pageable CPU `f32` only, with canonical `torch.cpu.is_available()` availability, invariant native `torch.is_vulkan_available() is False` build metadata, always-initialized `torch.cpu.is_initialized()` state, invariant `torch.cpu.current_device()` metadata, `torch.cpu.device_count()` metadata, eager no-op `torch.cpu.synchronize(device=None)`, cached CPU-aware `torch.get_device_module(device=None)` resolution, no-argument `Tensor.is_pinned` metadata, `Tensor.cpu` identity/channel-last conversion, `Tensor.get_device`/`torch.get_device` ordinal metadata, `Tensor.type()` legacy type-name introspection plus exact-identity `torch.float32`/`torch.float` and `"torch.FloatTensor"` conversions with strict-bool `non_blocking`, dtype-backed `torch.is_signed` introspection, read-only `torch.float32.abbr == "f32"` metadata, canonical `torch.float32.to_real()` identity, native float32-only `torch.finfo`, float32-only `torch.can_cast` and `torch.promote_types`, `TorchFunctionMode` dispatch for exact-native variadic `torch.atleast_2d` and `torch.atleast_3d` plus no-argument `Tensor.tanh`, and the unary `torch.overrides.has_torch_function_unary` dispatch probe; other `Tensor.type` targets and the deprecated `async` form, variadic operand overrides for `torch.atleast_2d` and `torch.atleast_3d`, plus generic and variadic probes remain unsupported |
| creation, elementwise, reductions | 15% | Python-integer `torch.broadcast_shapes` shape inference, basic arithmetic including distinct `torch.neg` and `torch.negative` builtins, inference-only top-level `torch.exp`, inference-only `Tensor.reciprocal`/`torch.reciprocal` through one native unary operation, and inference-only `Tensor.tanh`, reductions, and exact tensor equality |
| linear algebra and signal operations | 10% | Rank-2 float32 matmul plus the invariant `torch.get_float32_matmul_precision() == "highest"` query; reduced-precision `"high"`/`"medium"` modes and their setter remain unsupported |
| autograd and higher-order differentiation | 15% | Python-owned `Tensor.backward(gradient=None, retain_graph=None, create_graph=False, inputs=None)` with default-equivalent positional and keyword forms, plus zero- and single-root `torch.autograd.backward` and exact two-element tuple/list roots of one-element gradient-accumulating native leaves, including duplicate roots and matching exact tuple/list `grad_tensors` containing `None`, backed by the native scalar-root and joint leaf-root engines; exact empty tuple/list roots remain PyTorch-compatible no-ops with omitted, `None`, exact empty tuple/list, or exact singleton-`None` tuple/list gradients; read-only `Tensor.grad_dtype` metadata for supported float32 leaves; and leaf-only `Tensor.retain_grad()` no-op for gradient-requiring leaves, including empty tensors and leaf views created under `no_grad`; concrete gradients, retained or higher-order graphs, explicit `inputs`, Tensor backward override/mode dispatch, two-root inputs outside the one-element leaf subset, larger root sets, custom autograd sequences, mismatched gradient sequences, `grad_variables`, configurable gradient dtypes, retained non-leaf gradients, `autograd.grad`, and broader autograd APIs remain unsupported |
| neural-network functional API and modules | 15% | out-of-place `torch.nn.functional.relu` backed by the native ReLU kernel; rank-1 `torch.nn.functional.linear` for exact native CPU float32 tensors with `None` or a matching rank-1 bias through transpose, bias-seeded matmul accumulation, and squeeze, plus bias-free rank-2 and rank-3 inputs through transpose and matmul with rank-3 flatten and reshape; exact-identity dropout-family evaluation, zero-probability, and empty-input paths, including rank-2 `dropout1d` shared-storage views and exact rank-2 `dropout2d` identities; and deterministic out-of-place `torch.nn.functional.dropout`, `torch.nn.functional.dropout3d` for rank-5 inputs, and `torch.nn.functional.feature_alpha_dropout` training at `p=1` through native scalar multiplication |
| optimizers, initialization, data utilities | 5% | unsupported |
| serialization, state dictionaries, model interchange | 5% | Mutable process-global load-byte-order state through `torch.serialization.LoadEndianness`, `torch.serialization.get_default_load_endianness()`, and `torch.serialization.set_default_load_endianness(endianness)`; process-global archive checksum state through `torch.serialization.get_crc32_options()` and `torch.serialization.set_crc32_options(compute_crc32)`; plus mutable process-global mmap flags through `torch.serialization.get_default_mmap_options()` and the immediate/context-managed `torch.serialization.set_default_mmap_options(flags)` for `mmap.MAP_PRIVATE` and `mmap.MAP_SHARED`; saving, loading, state dictionaries, and model interchange remain unsupported |
| compilation, parallelism, distributed execution | 5% | Exact eager warning-only `torch.jit.strict_fusion()` no-op contexts; exact eager `torch.jit.Attribute(value, type)` value/type tuples; exact eager `torch.jit.isinstance(obj, target_type)` refinement checks for plain and parameterized container types, tuples of types, `Optional`, and `Union`, including empty-container warnings and invalid raw-container errors; invariant eager `torch.jit.onednn_fusion_enabled() is False` introspection; exact eager `torch.compiler.assume_constant_result(fn)` marking; direct-call and factory `torch.compiler.disable(fn=None, recursive=True, *, reason=None)` wrapping for Python functions with transparent eager calls, method binding, configured metadata with factory-time recursive snapshots, repeated wrapping, exceptions, and decorated-function pickling; mutable process-global default-backend state through `torch.compiler.set_default_backend(backend)` and `torch.compiler.get_default_backend()`, including exact string/callable identity, `None` reset to `"inductor"`, thread and module-reload visibility, and preservation across eager no-op `torch.compiler.reset()` calls; single-worker `torch.get_num_threads()` intra-op and `torch.get_num_interop_threads()` inter-op introspection; native false `torch.has_openmp`, `torch.has_mkl`, and `torch.has_lapack` build-capability flags plus canonical `torch.backends.openmp.is_available()`, `torch.backends.mkl.is_available()`, invariant-false `torch.backends.nnpack.is_available()`, CPU-only `torch.backends.cuda.is_built()`, and invariant-false `torch.backends.cudnn.is_available()` build queries; default-enabled `torch.autograd.is_multithreading_enabled()` state; eager-state `torch.compiler.is_compiling()`, `torch.compiler.is_dynamo_compiling()`, and `torch.compiler.is_exporting()`; local-tensor `Tensor.is_distributed()` metadata; package, Gloo, MPI, NCCL, UCC, and XCCL backend-capability `torch.distributed.is_available()`, `torch.distributed.is_gloo_available()`, `torch.distributed.is_mpi_available()`, `torch.distributed.is_nccl_available()`, `torch.distributed.is_ucc_available()`, and `torch.distributed.is_xccl_available()` queries; default-state `torch.distributed.is_initialized()`; zero-process-group `torch.distributed.get_pg_count()` introspection; and environment-backed `torch.distributed.get_node_local_rank(fallback_rank=None)` discovery; CUDA tensors, transfers, streams, events, kernels, and runtime availability, cuDNN version, configuration, and execution, OpenMP, MKL, and NNPACK configuration, verbosity, and execution, LAPACK backend namespaces, oneDNN fusion mutation, TorchScript modules, scripting, interfaces, tracing, fusion execution, autograd multithreading mutation, thread-count setters, compilation, export, `disable` context-manager use, class and builtin disabling, parallel execution, distributed tensor types, Gloo, MPI, NCCL, UCC, or XCCL initialization, process-group creation, global-rank and world-size access, backend execution, and collectives remain unsupported |
| ergonomics, diagnostics, documentation, ecosystem integration | 5% | Mutable process-global native warning policy through `torch.set_warn_always(b)` and `torch.is_warn_always_enabled()`, including strict bool validation, thread and reload visibility, PyTorch-compatible warn-once marker preservation, and isolation from ordinary Python `warnings.warn` sites |

Within an area, coverage includes ordinary use plus error behavior, empty tensors, numerical edge cases, non-contiguous layouts, and interactions with autograd and device/dtype dispatch. Newly supported cells are added to permanent regression and performance matrices; existing cells are not retired to improve a score.
